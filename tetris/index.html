How to Play Tetris

🎯 Objective

Stack falling tetromino pieces to create complete horizontal lines. When a line is filled, it disappears and you earn points!

🎮 Controls

Use arrow keys to move and rotate pieces. Space bar for instant drop. Master the controls for higher scores!

📈 Scoring

Clear multiple lines at once for bonus points! Higher levels give more points per line cleared.

🏆 Strategy

Plan ahead and leave space for the I-piece to clear 4 lines at once (Tetris) for maximum points!
